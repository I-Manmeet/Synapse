What Synapse is

A leadership team, made of AI agents.

Instead of one chatbot guessing at everything, Synapse has an analyst, a CFO, a market researcher, a customer analyst, a risk manager, and a strategist — coordinated by a manager. Business owners have data but still struggle with "what should I actually do?" Dashboards show numbers; a single chatbot answers in one shot. Synapse breaks the decision into its parts, diagnoses the cause, weighs the trade-offs, and recommends.

How it actually works
A user asks: "Our sales dropped 20%. Why, and what should we do?"
Press play to watch the agents work through it.

Synapse sends the business question through its specialist agents — Analytics, Finance, Market, and Customer — then passes their findings to Risk and Strategy before the Manager produces one final report. This coordinated pipeline lets each agent focus on a different part of the business decision.
